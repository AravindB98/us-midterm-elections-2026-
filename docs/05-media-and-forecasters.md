## 5.7 Media literacy checklist

1. Check the poll's date, sample size, likely vs registered voters, and pollster track record.
2. Compare a single poll to the average, not to the last poll you saw.
3. A projection or "call" is not certification.
4. Treat social-media claims about fraud or "rigged" counts skeptically until confirmed by election officials or courts.
5. Remember partisan sponsorship: campaign internal polls are released when they help.
