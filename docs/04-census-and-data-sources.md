# 4. Census and Official Data Sources

[Back to README](../README.md)

## 4.1 US Census Bureau

| Dataset | What it gives you | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Voting and Registration (CPS November Supplement) | Turnout and registration by age, race, sex, education, income, citizenship and state for every federal election since 1964; the standard source for "who votes" | [census.gov/topics/public-sector/voting.html](https://www.census.gov/topics/public-sector/voting.html) |
| 2020 Census apportionment | House seats per state for 2022-2030 elections | [2020 apportionment data](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2020/dec/2020-apportionment-data.html) |
| Redistricting Data Program (P.L. 94-171) | Block-level population used to draw districts | [Redistricting data office](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/about/rdo.html) |
| Citizen Voting Age Population (CVAP) special tabulation | Citizens 18+ by race/ethnicity at small geographies; used in Voting Rights Act cases | [CVAP](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/decennial-census/about/voting-rights/cvap.html) |
| American Community Survey (ACS) | Annual demographics: income, education, language, foreign-born and citizenship status, by congressional district | [ACS](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/acs) |
| My Congressional District | Quick ACS profile for any district | [census.gov/mycd](https://www.census.gov/mycd/) |
| data.census.gov | Search and download all tables | [data.census.gov](https://data.census.gov/) |
| Census API | Programmatic access | [census.gov/data/developers](https://www.census.gov/data/developers.html) |
| Population estimates | Annual state and county estimates, early signal for the 2030 reapportionment | [Population estimates](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/popest.html) |

Tip: The CPS voting supplement for the 2026 election is typically released the following spring/summer, so 2026 turnout-by-demographic tables will appear in 2027.

Why non-citizens matter to the census: the census counts all residents, citizens or not, and those counts determine House seats and electoral votes. Whether to exclude non-citizens from apportionment is a live political and legal debate.

## 4.2 Federal election agencies

| Source | What it is | Link |
|---|---|---|
| Election Assistance Commission (EAC) | Election Administration and Voting Survey (EAVS): registration, mail ballots, provisional ballots, polling places by jurisdiction | [eac.gov/research-and-data](https://www.eac.gov/research-and-data/studies-and-reports) |
| Federal Election Commission (FEC) | Candidate and PAC fundraising and spending; official federal election results compilations | [fec.gov/data](https://www.fec.gov/data/) |
| Clerk of the House | Official statistics of each congressional election | [clerk.house.gov election statistics](https://history.house.gov/Institution/Election-Statistics/Election-Statistics/) |
| US Senate historical office | Senate classes, history, party division | [senate.gov party division](https://www.senate.gov/history/partydiv.htm) |
| Congress.gov | Text and status of election and immigration bills | [congress.gov](https://www.congress.gov/) |
| vote.gov | Official voter registration portal (citizens only) | [vote.gov](https://vote.gov/) |
| CISA election security | Federal election infrastructure security | [cisa.gov/topics/election-security](https://www.cisa.gov/topics/election-security) |

## 4.3 State and local sources (official results)

- Each state's Secretary of State or election board publishes official results and certifies winners. Directory: [NASS Can I Vote](https://www.nass.org/can-I-vote) and [USA.gov state election offices](https://www.usa.gov/election-office).
- Massachusetts: [sec.state.ma.us/divisions/elections](https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/elections/elections-and-voting.htm)
- National Conference of State Legislatures election law database: [ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns](https://www.ncsl.org/elections-and-campaigns)
