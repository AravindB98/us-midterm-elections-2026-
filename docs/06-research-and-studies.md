# 6. Election Research and Studies

[Back to README](../README.md)

Ongoing academic and policy research on US elections. Think tanks are labeled with their commonly described orientation so readers can balance perspectives.

## 6.1 Large academic surveys

| Study | Host | What it studies | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cooperative Election Study (CES, formerly CCES) | Harvard University (consortium of 60+ universities) | 50,000+ respondent pre/post-election survey; vote choice, turnout validated against voter files | [cces.gov.harvard.edu](https://cces.gov.harvard.edu/) |
| American National Election Studies (ANES) | Stanford and University of Michigan | Gold-standard long-run study of voter attitudes since 1948 | [electionstudies.org](https://electionstudies.org/) |
| Survey of the Performance of American Elections (SPAE) | MIT Election Data + Science Lab | Voter experience: wait times, confidence, problems at polls | [electionlab.mit.edu/research/projects/survey-performance-american-elections](https://electionlab.mit.edu/research/projects/survey-performance-american-elections) |
| AP VoteCast | Associated Press with NORC at UChicago | Large election-week survey of who voted and why (replacement for exit polls at AP) | [ap.org/votecast](https://www.ap.org/votecast/) |
| Exit polls (National Election Pool) | Edison Research for ABC, CBS, CNN, NBC | Election-day and early-voter interviews | [edisonresearch.com](https://www.edisonresearch.com/) |
| Pew Research Center validated voter studies | Pew | Who voted, matched to voter files | [pewresearch.org/politics](https://www.pewresearch.org/politics/) |
| Gallup | Gallup | Party ID, presidential approval, institutional trust | [news.gallup.com/topic/politics](https://news.gallup.com/topic/politics.aspx) |

## 6.4 Key research questions in 2026

| Question | Where research is happening |
|---|---|
| Does mid-decade redistricting change House control? | Voting Rights Lab, Ballotpedia, Redistricting Data Hub, Cook |
| How common is noncitizen registration/voting? | BPC (Utah audit: 27 confirmed of 2M+), Brennan Center, Heritage database, state audits |
| Effects of proof-of-citizenship laws on eligible voters | Brennan Center, BPC, Kansas experience studies |
| Polling accuracy after 2016/2020 misses | AAPOR, Pew, Silver Bulletin |
| Midterm penalty theory ("surge and decline", referendum on president) | Political science literature (e.g. James Campbell, Erikson); applied in Northeastern faculty analysis |
| Ranked-choice voting outcomes | MEDSL, FairVote (pro-RCV), Alaska/Maine studies |
| Election official safety and federal pressure | CEIR, Brennan Center, NBC reporting on court orders |
| Immigration as a voting issue among immigrant-origin citizens | Carnegie (Indian Americans), Pew (Asian and Latino voters) |
